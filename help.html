PharmaB2B · Buyer Guide

Everything you need to start ordering in minutes.

PharmaB2B is the wholesale ordering portal for Sanimed Pharma. Distributors, LGUs, hospitals and retail partners use it to check live stock and prices, build an order, and pay through a normal bank transfer — an admin team then verifies your payment and ships your products. Every screen in this guide is an exact copy of what you will see.

The big picture

How an order moves through the platform

There are only two sides: you (the buyer) and the Sanimed admin team. You handle the first four steps — creating your account, picking products, placing the order and uploading proof of payment. The admin team handles the last three — approving your payment, shipping and delivering. This one diagram is the entire system:

Account approved → Browse products → Place order → Pay via bank transfer → Upload proof → Admin verifies → Shipped → Delivered
Good to know There is no online card payment. Settlement is offline: you pay by bank transfer, then upload a photo or PDF of the deposit slip (we call it the “payment proof”). An admin checks it manually before your order is processed.

Step 1 · Your account

Create your buyer account

Anyone from a distributor, LGU, hospital or retail outlet can register. You only need your business details and a delivery location pin. Self-registration always creates a Buyer account — buyer accounts first need admin approval before you can sign in.

  1. Open the sign-in page and tap “Create account”.The page opens on “Sign in”. Click the Create account tab on top of the white card to open the registration form.
  2. Fill in your details.Full name, business name, business type (Distributor / LGU / Hospital / Other), phone, email and delivery address. The email must be one you can access.
  3. Pin your delivery location on the map.This is required. Move the map and click the exact spot where you want delivery. Your coordinates appear under the map.
  4. Choose a strong password.At least 10 characters, with an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, a number and a special character — for example MedSupply@2026.
  5. Tap “Create account”.That's it. A confirmation screen explains the next step: an administrator must approve your account.

Step 5 · Shopping

Browse the product catalog

The Products screen lists every active item with the live price and current stock. Cards are color-coded: green edge = in stock, amber edge = only a few left, red edge = out of stock.

Searching and filtering

  • Search box — type at least 3 characters of the product name or SKU (e.g. para or PAR-500). Results filter automatically as you type.
  • Category dropdown — narrow the list to one category, like Analgesics or Vitamins.
  • Pagination — “Showing 1–24 of 1,024 products” and ← Prev / Next → buttons at the bottom.

Reading a product card

  • SKU — the code in amber at the top-left (useful for searching, e.g. PAR-500-BX).
  • Stock badge — “In stock”, “Only X left”, or “Out of stock”.
  • Strength · unit — e.g. “500mg · box” tells you the dosage and how it is packed.
  • Price — the price per unit (per box, bottle, or strip as shown).

Good to know Cards marked Out of stock cannot be added to an order — the button is disabled. Stock is reserved for you the moment you place your order, so a product you just ordered shows as reduced for everyone else.

Step 6 · Build your order

Place an order

You build your order on the Place Order screen: search or filter the catalog, set quantities, and review the totals before confirming. No payment is taken here — ordering is free until you pay by bank transfer afterwards.

  1. Open “Place Order” from the menu.The screen opens with the same searchable catalog, but now each product has a quantity box with − / + buttons.
  2. Set your quantities.Tap + to add units up to the available stock. The subtotal updates instantly at the bottom of the page.
  3. Review the summary.The right side (or below, on a phone) shows Subtotal, Delivery and the Order total. The delivery estimate is pre-calculated from your pinned location to the Sanimed warehouse.
  4. Add a note (optional).Type anything you want Sanimed to know in the “Notes for this order” box — e.g. delivery times, contact person, landmarks.
  5. Tap “Place Order”.Your order is created instantly with the status Pending Payment. You will be moved to Order History, where your new order is already waiting at the top.

Step 7 · Payment proof

Pay by bank transfer, then upload your proof

After placing an order, actually pay the total to Sanimed's bank account (details are given to you by your Sanimed contact). Then upload proof — a clear photo of the deposit slip, a screenshot of the mobile transfer, or the PDF receipt — so the admin team can verify it.

  1. Go to “Order History”.Your new order is at the top with status Pending Payment. Open it, then tap Upload Proof.
  2. Choose your payment file.Click the file box and select a JPG, PNG or PDF image of your bank receipt — max 5 MB.
  3. Add an optional transaction reference.The bank reference or control number (e.g. BPI-0928394). This helps matching happen faster.
  4. Tap “Upload Proof”.Your order flips to Payment Uploaded — the Sanimed team is now reviewing it.

Step 8 · Understand the statuses

Every order status, explained simply

Your order is always in exactly one of these states. You can see it as a colored badge in Order History.

Pending Payment → Payment Uploaded → Approved → Shipped → Delivered
All statuses Pending Payment Payment Uploaded Approved Shipped Delivered Payment Rejected Cancelled

What each status means for you

  • Pending Payment — your order is reserved but you haven't uploaded your payment proof yet. You can still cancel.
  • Payment Uploaded — you submitted your proof; Sanimed's admin is checking it against the bank records.
  • Approved — your payment is verified. Your order is confirmed and queued for dispatch. Cancellation is no longer possible.
  • Shipped — your order left the warehouse; it's on the way to your delivery pin.
  • Delivered — done! The driver completed delivery. You'll see the full route history for the order.
  • Payment Rejected — the proof couldn't be verified (reason given). Stock is released back; upload a better proof to re-hold it, or cancel.
  • Cancelled — you cancelled (only allowed while Pending Payment or Payment Rejected). Stock you reserved is released.

Good to know

Delivery fees & rules

Delivery is computed from your delivery pin to the Sanimed warehouse, and shown to you before you place the order so there are no surprises.

  • Distance-based charge — the fee is distance (from warehouse to your pin) × a rate per meter set by Sanimed.
  • Free delivery — if Sanimed set free-delivery terms, orders that reach the minimum amount AND are within the maximum distance get ₱0 delivery.
  • No charge conditions — if no rate is configured, or your location can't be measured, delivery is ₱0.
  • Always previewed — the “Delivery” line in Place Order is the same number applied to your order. What the app estimates is what you pay.
Tip Double-check that your delivery pin is exactly where you want the goods sent — the fee (and the driver) go to that spot. Pin a wrong spot and you may be billed for a longer route.

Troubleshooting

Common questions & help

I registered, but I can't sign in.

Your account is Pending Approval. This is normal — an admin must approve you first. The sign-in screen shows a clear message for this instead of pretending the password is wrong. Wait for approval, then try again.

How do I know which bank account to pay?

Sanimed's bank details come from your Sanimed contact or the onboarding pack. PharmaB2B handles the proof side — you upload the receipt after transferring.

My payment was rejected. What now?

Tap View on the order to read the admin's rejection reason, then upload a clearer proof (or the correct receipt). The order returns to Payment Uploaded for a new review. You can also cancel the order if you changed your mind.

Can I cancel an order?

Yes — but only when the status is Pending Payment or Payment Rejected. Once your payment is Approved, the order is locked and can no longer be cancelled.

My account was blocked.

Sign-in will simply fail. A blocked account usually means an admin deactivated it for review; contact Sanimed to resolve it.

What upload types are accepted?

JPG, PNG and PDF, at most 5 MB each. Images are compressed automatically; PDFs are previewed safely. If the file is too big, take a smaller photo or convert to PDF.

Is my payment information safe?

Yes. There is no card or full banking data stored. Proofs are stored in a private area, never linked on public pages, and only the buyer and admins can view them.

I've done all this and still need help.

Contact your Sanimed representative or support desk with your email address and order number (e.g. ORD-20260918-A1B2C3D4E5F6) so they can locate your account instantly.
